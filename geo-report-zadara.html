⚠ Critical Issues
▼
1
Missing Security Headers (Score: 4/10)
No HSTS, Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, or Referrer-Policy headers detected. This is a trust signal deficit — search engines and AI platforms factor security posture into authority assessment. Fix: Configure security headers in Cloudflare or WP Engine settings.
2
No IndexNow Protocol
IndexNow enables near-instant Bing indexing, directly impacting ChatGPT and Copilot visibility. Fix: Install the IndexNow WordPress plugin — takes under 5 minutes and benefits two AI platforms.
3
UTM Parameters in Sitemap
Sitemap contains URLs with UTM tracking parameters, risking duplicate content indexing. Fix: Remove all UTM-tagged URLs from the sitemap and ensure canonical tags handle parameter variations.
⚠ Warnings
▼
1
Short Cache TTL (max-age=600)
Static assets cached for only 10 minutes. Should be 1 year (31536000s) with content-hashed filenames for CSS/JS/images. HTML can remain short-cached.
2
Elementor JS-Dependent Widgets
Some content rendered via Elementor may not be visible to AI crawlers that don't execute JavaScript. WP Engine's server-side rendering covers most content, but interactive widgets may be invisible to GPTBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot.
3
Blog Pagination Gaps
Sitemap shows pagination jumps (page/2/, page/3/, then page/43/), suggesting incomplete blog archive indexing. Verify all 45 pages of blog content are accessible and indexed.
